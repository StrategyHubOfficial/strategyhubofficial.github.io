# Studio Booking Guide

The StrategyHub studio is a broadcast-quality space for Bitcoin content creation.

## Features

- 4K video recording
- Multi-input audio mixing
- Professional acoustic treatment
- Technical assistance available (10,000 sats/hour)

## Booking Studio Time

1. Navigate to the [Studio](/dashboard/studio/) page
2. Click "Book Studio Time"
3. Select your start and end times
4. Choose any additional equipment needed
5. Optionally request technical assistance
6. Submit your booking

## Equipment

Studio default equipment is automatically included in your booking. You can also select additional equipment from the dropdown.

## Technical Assistance

If you need help during your session, check the "I need technical assistance" box. This costs 10,000 sats per hour and will be charged via OpenNode.

## Payment

Technical assistance requires payment before your booking is confirmed. You'll receive a payment link via OpenNode.

## Cancellation

Contact an admin if you need to cancel or reschedule your booking.
