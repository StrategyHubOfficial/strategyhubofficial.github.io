# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### What is StrategyHub?

StrategyHub is a free co-working space for advancing Bitcoin innovation and adoption, hosted by Strategy (the world's first Bitcoin treasury company).

### Who can join?

Bitcoin developers, researchers, and builders working on meaningful projects. Apply at [strategy.com/hub](https://strategy.com/hub).

### Is it really free?

Yes! StrategyHub is free for approved members.

## Studio

### How do I book studio time?

Go to the [Studio](/dashboard/studio/) page and click "Book Studio Time". Select your time slot and any additional equipment.

### What's included in a studio booking?

All default studio equipment is automatically included. You can also request additional equipment.

### How much does technical assistance cost?

Technical assistance costs 10,000 satoshis per hour, payable via OpenNode.

## Projects

### Can anyone create a project?

Yes. Members can create projects from the dashboard. Contact an admin if you need help getting started.

### How do I get my project in the portfolio?

When creating or editing a project, check "Show my project in the hub portfolio". Only public, active, or completed projects appear in the portfolio.

### Can I donate to projects?

Yes! Click "Donate" on any project to support it with Bitcoin via OpenNode.

## Equipment

### What equipment is available?

Browse the [Resources](/dashboard/resources/) page to see all available equipment.

### Can I take equipment home?

Borrowable equipment can be checked out and taken off-site. Studio equipment must stay in the studio.

### How long can I keep equipment?

Checkout periods vary. See the return date when you request equipment.

## Support

### Need help?

Contact an admin through the dashboard or email support.

### Found a bug?

Report issues through the dashboard or contact the development team.
