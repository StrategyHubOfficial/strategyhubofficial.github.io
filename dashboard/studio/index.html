Studio Rules & Guidelines

  • Bookings must be made at least 24 hours in advance
  • Maximum booking duration is 8 hours per day
  • Studio equipment is included by default (cameras, lighting, audio)
  • Additional equipment can be requested during booking
  • Technical assistance is available at 10,000 sats/hour
  • Please cancel at least 4 hours before your booking if you cannot make it
  • Clean up after your session and return all equipment to its place
  • Respect other members' bookings and time slots
